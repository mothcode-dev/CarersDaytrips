About Us

Carer's Daytrips is all about you - the carer! The idea originated from carers needing and deserving a break. Often, carers are not adequately compensated for their time and dedication, therefore this website aims to make up for that!

Sometimes we're asked - what is a carer? While definitions vary, we found an overlap between definitions. We like to define a carer as someone (at any age) who provides unpaid care and support for any other individual, who is disabled, has an illness or chronic condition.

Based in the UK, we offer services primarily around London. These services include transport tracking (specifically TfL tracking), as well as day trip planning features.

What We Do

We help carers find and plan daytrips, across the UK. Whether you're looking to go to museums, see a show at the theatre, go on a peaceful walk in nature, or attend specialised support groups, we have you covered.

Trip Planning

Our trip planner tool helps you find the perfect trip according to your needs! With filtering options based off location, activity type, price and distance, you can pick the best option for you.

Accessibility Focused

Whenever you search for day trip options, we include filtering options for distance. This provides an easy way to see how long the trip will take, so that you can take that into account whenever making plans.

Live Tracking

We provide TfL live tracking functionality, allowing you to account for any closures or line suspensions. This will ensure that you can plan and make your journey with accurate, up-to-date information.

Affordable

One of the most important priorities to us is to ensure that you are aware of activity costs, and that you can plan with money in mind. We showcase many free and low-cost activities, so you can still have fun, without the financial stress.
